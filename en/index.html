In one sentence

The right inventory, the right audience, at the right price — automatically, one impression at a time.

No more insertion orders negotiated by hand. Each impression is assessed individually based on its context, the audience seeing it and the campaign objective. Every publisher becomes reachable from a single entry point.

01

Speed

The impression is auctioned, bought and served in under 200 ms, while the page is loading.

02

Scope

Display, video, audio, CTV and DOOH are all bought from the same platform, across five screens.

03

Unified data

The same profile is recognised across every publisher, and can be enriched with third-party data.

04

Global frequency capping

Repeat exposure is managed globally, not publisher by publisher.
