Chapter 00

Entry quiz

Ten questions with four options each, and only one right answer every time. Nothing is at stake: the goal is to spot, as a group, what needs clarifying before getting into the content.

How to run it

Out loud, let everyone answer before revealing. Questions 4, 5 and 10 split the room the most: they make good starting points for discussion.

What the score measures

Nothing individual. When most people get the same question wrong, it flags a concept to revisit in the session — not a lack of skill.

Where to find the answers

Each explanation points to a section of the material. The glossary, open in a second tab, is your safety net for the whole session.
