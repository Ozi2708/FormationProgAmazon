2.1 — Objectives and KPIs

Always start from the objective, never from the format

Three families of objectives, each with its own metrics. The format, the screen and the deal follow from that choice — not the other way round.

Awareness
Be seen by the right audience: reach, asset viewability, exposure frequency.
CPMReachFrequency
Engagement
Drive qualified traffic: visits, key page views, time spent, interactions.
CPCCTRVisits
Conversion
Deliver a business outcome: leads, sales, revenue, profitability.
CPLCPAROAS
The rule: one primary objective per campaign. The other metrics help you read performance, not optimise it. A line item chasing two objectives serves neither well.
2.2 — Data

Which data to identify the audience

At Amazon Ads

Amazon Audiences are built on first-party shopping, browsing and streaming signals, and can only be activated in Amazon DSP. On top of that come advertiser audiences (hashed CRM, remarketing) and third-party audiences as an extra layer.

Amazon Audiences carry no additional data CPM fee.

GDPR — the question comes up at the brief, not at launch. Any activation requires a compliant legal basis and consent. Targeting that is approved on the media side but blocked on the legal side costs a week of flight time, and the credibility that goes with it.

Display

CPM · CTR
Video

2.4 — Setup

From brief to go live

Setup is structured on three levels. Most of the steering happens at line item level.

Level 1

The campaign

Objective, total budget, flight dates, reference KPI, market.

Level 2 · the core

The line item, or strategy

Audience, allowed inventory and deals, bid type and maximum CPM, frequency cap, device type, geography, dayparting.

Level 3

The creatives

Formats and variants, DCO, tracking tags and pixels, landing pages.

2.6 — Measurement and wrap-up

Three levels of reading, in this order

Media level

Did we buy well?

Impressions, reach and frequency, viewability, completion, actual CPM by environment.

Behaviour level

Did we trigger a reaction?

CTR, visits, key page views, time spent, add-to-carts, branded searches.

Business level

Did we deliver a result?

Sales, revenue, cost per acquisition, ROAS, share of new customers.

Beyond the last click

You have post-test and brand lift studies, incrementality measurement and cross-dataset analysis in a clean room — at Amazon Ads through Amazon Marketing Cloud, which lets you match media exposure with purchase behaviour without exposing individual-level data.

The wrap-up is prepared at the brief. KPIs, measurement sources and methodology are set before launch — not pieced together afterwards.

Post-test and brand lift

Measure the shift in brand perception and recall in an exposed group versus a control group.

Incrementality measurement

Isolate what the media produced on top of what would have happened without it.

Clean room (AMC)

Match exposure and purchase, build custom audiences, without ever exposing individual-level data.

Key takeaways — chapter 2
A campaign is built in order: objective, data, inventory, setup, optimisation, measurement.
  • One primary objective per campaign, one line item per objective, one KPI per line item.
  • First-party data is the most reliable; third-party data is paid on a CPM basis, as an extra layer.
  • Setup happens at line item level — that is where most of the steering is done.
  • Optimise budget and bids first, creative comes later. One variable at a time.
  • The wrap-up is prepared at the brief: KPIs, sources and methodology set before launch.
