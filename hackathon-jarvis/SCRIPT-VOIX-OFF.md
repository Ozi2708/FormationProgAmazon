### Texte d'un seul tenant (pour t'entraîner)

> Nine a.m. For a Programmatic Solutions Consultant, the day starts with emails, Slack, invites, DSP exports, tickets and product updates. All at once. Every answer already exists. It's just scattered.
> Sixteen thousand emails. Seven hundred DSP updates. Forty live campaigns. And one consultant.
>
> What if all of it lived in one place? So I built J.A.R.V.I.S. One AI brain, powered by Kiro, that turns all of it into a single place to work.
>
> Every morning, a thirty-second briefing tells me where to start: mail, meetings, campaigns. My inbox arrives already sorted. What concerns me comes first. Every thread waiting on a reply resurfaces. One click, and Kiro drafts the follow-up, in my voice. And every meeting is linked to its client, with the prep already done.
>
> Then, the campaigns. DSP exports feed the brain natively. The pacing map shows at a glance what's behind, what to watch, and what's on pace. I see under-delivery before my client does. And numbers become actions: two percent delivered, forty-one thousand euros at risk, ends November fifteenth. That's now a task, with the next step. When delivery breaks, troubleshooting is guided: TWIG, Waypoint, the twenty-four-hour window, and every SIM ticket tracked.
>
> My whole portfolio is ranked by urgency. One click, and I get the full client picture: risk, next step, latest emails, live campaigns and their curves. On one screen.
>
> Seven hundred product updates? Kiro summarizes them, flags what needs action from me, and prepares a recap every Thursday. Slack is connected too, so nothing gets lost.
>
> And because it knows everything, it produces. One sentence, and my PowerPoint for Thursday's team meeting is ready. Meeting preps are written the night before, without me asking. And my weekly business review writes itself, from what actually happened this week.
>
> I stay in control. Drafts land in Outlook, but nothing is ever sent on its own. And every correction I make teaches it how I work.
>
> Next: Salesforce, for sales and targets. Then connected brains, sharing what they know, so the whole team moves together.
>
> Stop searching. Start consulting. This is J.A.R.V.I.S.

### Conseils d'enregistrement

- Enregistre la voix **en regardant la vidéo** (la bande-son est déjà mixée assez bas, autour de -20 LUFS, pour laisser la place à ta voix).
- Dans ton outil de montage, pose la voix vers -16 LUFS. Si la musique te gêne, baisse la piste vidéo de 3 à 6 dB, ou repars des pistes séparées `music.wav` et `sfx.wav`.
- Les deux moments à ne pas rater : « So I built **J.A.R.V.I.S.** » sur le flash (0:31) et « Stop searching. Start consulting. » sur les deux impacts (2:54 et 2:55).
