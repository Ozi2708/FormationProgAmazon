### Texte d'un seul tenant (pour t'entraîner)

> Nine a.m. Emails, Slack, invites, DSP exports, product updates… By eleven, we are still catching up. Everything we need is in there. No one can read it all.
>
> So we built J.A.R.V.I.S., the AI co-pilot of the PSC. It reads everything: mail, Slack, calendar, DSP exports, tickets, docs. It knows what matters. And it does the work. One brain. One platform.
>
> Every morning, ninety emails become three lines. Every DSP export is read. Under-delivery is caught first, and becomes a costed task. It writes like me, grounded in live data. I add a note in French: it rewrites it. A dedicated agent preps every meeting the day before. One sentence: the deck and the WBR are ready. And everything else lives in the same platform.
>
> Add it up: eight hours back, every week. One working day per PSC. We stay in control: nothing is ever sent on its own. And it learns from every email, document and campaign result. Next: Salesforce, then connected brains for the whole team. Stop searching. Start consulting. This is J.A.R.V.I.S.

### Conseils pour le direct

- Si tu prends du retard, saute une phrase : chaque scène se comprend seule.
- Les heures gagnées (2 h, 1 h 30, 2 h, 1 h, 1 h 30 = 8 h) sont des **estimations** : dis-le simplement (« on estime… »).
- Les deux moments à ne pas rater : « So we built **J.A.R.V.I.S.** » sur le flash, et « Stop searching. Start consulting. » sur les deux impacts de la fin.
